Helpful things to include

To help us look into any issues, it's useful if you can mention:

  • What happened and what you expected to happen
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings > General > About)
  • Any steps to reproduce the problem

Common questions

The app is asking for Health permissions

TredMarx needs access to Apple Health to save your treadmill workouts. You can manage these permissions in Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > TredMarx.

My step count seems wrong

TredMarx uses your device's built-in motion sensors. Step counting accuracy can vary depending on how you carry your phone while on the treadmill. Holding it or placing it in a pocket generally gives the best results.
